The SMILES Concept

SMILES (Simplified Molecular-Input Line-Entry System) writes a molecule as one line of text. Click any example to see it drawn.

🔤

1 · Atoms

Organic atoms are written by symbol: C, N, O, S, P, F, Cl, Br, I. Hydrogens are filled in automatically.

CCO → ethanol
🔗

2 · Bonds

Single bonds are implied. Use = for double, # for triple. Neighbours in the string are bonded.

C=CC#N → acrylonitrile
🌿

3 · Branches

Parentheses mark a side chain that attaches to the atom just before them.

CC(C)C(=O)O → isobutyric acid
⭕

4 · Rings

Matching digits close a ring: the two atoms carrying the same digit are bonded together.

C1CCCCC1 → cyclohexane
🧿

5 · Aromaticity

Lowercase letters mark aromatic atoms. A ring of lowercase c is a benzene-type ring.

c1ccc2ccccc2c1 → naphthalene
[ ]

6 · Brackets

Square brackets give explicit details such as charge or hydrogen count, for example [NH4+] or [Na+].

C[N+](C)(C)C → tetramethylammonium
